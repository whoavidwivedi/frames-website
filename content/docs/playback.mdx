## Move through a film

Press **Space** to play or pause. Use the left and right arrow keys to skip, or drag the timeline to a precise position. Press **F** to enter fullscreen and **Esc** to leave it.

## Choose a track

Open the captions menu in the playback controls to select an audio or subtitle track. In a narrow window these options move into the overflow menu. Available languages depend on the tracks included in the file.

## Surround dialogue on stereo speakers

Frames 2.0.8 uses the speaker order reported by the Dolby decoder. When a 5.1 soundtrack plays through two speakers, the center channel feeds both sides. If dialogue consistently comes from the left on an older version, update and replay the same scene.
